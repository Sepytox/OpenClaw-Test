About Kawasaki

Kawasaki is one of the oldest and most renowned motorcycle manufacturers in the world. Known for performance, innovation, and reliability for over 100 years.

⚡ Fastest Kawasaki Motorcycle

Kawasaki Ninja H2

With a top speed of over 300 km/h, the H2 is one of the fastest production motorcycles in the world. The supercharged engine delivers a massive 231 HP.

🐢 Slowest Kawasaki Motorcycle

Kawasaki KLX 110

This small dirt bike is perfect for beginners and children. With only a 113cc engine and a top speed of ~90 km/h, it is safe and reliable.

🆕 Newest Kawasaki Model

Kawasaki Ninja 1200 Hybrid (2026)

The latest innovation with hybrid technology. Combining electric and petrol drive for maximum efficiency and performance.

⏰ Oldest Kawasaki Motorcycle

Kawasaki Samurai (1960s)

The oldest Kawasaki motorcycle dates back to the 1960s. Classic design and robust engineering that is still appreciated to this day.

Kawasaki — Tradition Meets the Future

From classic models to high-tech superbikes — Kawasaki remains one of the top destinations for motorcycle enthusiasts worldwide.
